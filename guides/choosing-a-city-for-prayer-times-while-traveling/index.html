How to Choose the Right City for Prayer Times While Traveling

Published August 3, 2026 · 4 min read

When you travel, choose the city where you will actually be praying—or the closest practical city to your accommodation. Prayer times are calculated for a location, not simply for a country name. Sunrise, sunset, and the daily prayer times change gradually with place and season.

Start with the city where you are staying

If you are staying in a hotel or with family, search for that city directly on CityTimeHub. If your accommodation is near a large city or within its urban area, use the city that your location is practically part of.

There is no advantage in choosing a distant, better-known city in the same country. The closer city gives a more useful view of local time, sunrise, sunset, and the day’s prayer schedule.

Do not confuse local time with a time difference

Two cities in one country may share a time zone, but a large country can also have more than one. Some places change their clocks seasonally, so the difference between two cities can change during the year.

A city page shows the time and date for that city’s local time zone. To follow several places, use the Cities Board; for travel plans or calls between countries, use Time Difference.

Check the calculation approach when needed

Prayer times use the city’s location and the calculation method followed there. You may therefore notice a limited difference between two timetables, especially for Fajr and Isha, or in places at higher latitudes.

For a congregation, the announced timetable of the nearby mosque remains the practical reference. The website helps you follow the day’s times and changes, while Monthly Prayer Times is useful for planning ahead.

When you move between two cities on the same day

If you leave one city and arrive in another, change the city after arrival or before the next prayer. Do not rely on the departure city for the whole day, especially when the journey crosses time zones.

Before the trip, add both places to the Cities Board and compare the local time and next prayer. It gives you a clear picture of the arrival time without manual calculations.

A simple conclusion

Choose the city closest to where you are, check the local time and next prayer, and follow the mosque’s timetable for a congregation. In most journeys, that is all you need for clear and practical prayer times.
